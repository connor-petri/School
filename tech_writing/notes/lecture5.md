# Lecture 5 - Plagerism

---

## What is Plagerism?
- Plagerism is the act of using someone else's work or ideas without giving proper credit.
- It can include copying text, ideas, images, or any other form of intellectual property.

### The Negative Effects of Plagerism
- Original work disrespected
- It becomes impossible to follow the "train of thought", complication or stymying further research
- It is unfair (a "short cut" or a way to avoid doing work)
- Consequences for students can include:
    - Failing the assignment
    - Failing the course
    - Academic probation
    - Expulsion from the institution

### Why Does is Matter in Computer Science?
- Public trust in enhanced by vigorous and visible adherence to ethical principles
- Plagerism can lead to a loss of credibility and trust in the field
- It can also lead to legal consequences for those who engage in it

### How to Avoid Plagiarism
- Cite whenever you:
    - Paraphrase the ideas of others
    - Directly quote the words of others
    - Refer to data or data sets
    - Reprint or adapt a table or figure, even ones covered by Creative Commons licenses

## Types of Plagiarism
- Unacknowledged summarizing
- Copying material
- Unacknowledged paraphrasing
- Translating
- Taking ideas
- Collusion

### Self-Plagiarism
- Self-plagiarism is the act of reusing one's own work without proper citation or acknowledgment
- It can include:
    - Submitting the same work for multiple assignments
    - Reusing parts of previous work without citation
    - Publishing the same research in multiple venues without proper disclosure
