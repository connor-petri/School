## Task and Audience Analysis
- Task analysis can help deterine what your purpose is
### Three general purposes for communication in the workplace:
1. To create a record
    - Will it be referred to often?
    - Is it a legal document?
2. To give or request information
    - Is supplying/recieving the information time-critical?
    - How reliable is the information?
    - How exactly will the information be used?
3. To persuade or motivate action
    - Is the issue one of policy? Safety? Stratey?
    - Are there diverse opiinions about the issue?
    - How strongly help are those opinions?

### Task Analysis Components
### Writer
- Identify who will be writing the document (mostly you)
- Motivation - why are you writing this document?
- Bias - how do you feel about the thing you are writing about?
- Knowledge/experience - what do you know, and what is you r experience of the thing you are writing about?
- Role - what is your position within the organization?

### Audience
- The single most important aspect of audience/task analysis.
- Analyze the characteristics and needs of the audience
- Is your audience:
    - Internal/external?
    - Lateral/upstream/downstream?
    - Primary/secondary/tertiary?
- Consider the document's lifespan - will the audience change over time?

#### Audience Profile Sheet
- An easy way of anticipating and defining problems before they arise
- Allows you to thik more deeply about:
    - Your audience's identity
    - Your audience's competencies
    - Your audience's needs
    - Possible barriers to communication
- Adapt audience profile sheets for your own purposes

### Message
- Aligned with your purpose
- No extraneous information
- Targeted for your audience
- Professional and expressed in an appropriate tone
