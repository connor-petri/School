# Lecture 2: Technical Communication as a Solution
### Engineering Design Process
1. Define the Problem
2. Establish Criteria and Constraints
3. Brainstorm Possible Solutions
4. Research
5. Consider Alternative Solutions
6. Select a Promising Solution
7. Develop a Proposal
8. Prototype, Test, and Refine the Design
9. Communicate the Results

## Hyman's Problem Formulation Model
1. Need Statement
    - Recognizes and describes the need for a solution or improvement to an "unsatisfactory situation."
    - What is the problem that needs to be solved? 
    - What is wrong with the current situation?
2. Goal Statement
    - Describes the desired outcome or end state that will resolve the problem identified in the need statement.
    - What will the situation look like when the problem is solved?
3. Objectives
    - Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) criteria that define how to achieve the goal.
    - What specific targets or milestones need to be met to reach the goal? 
    - Define the quality and performance standards for the solution.
4. Constriants
    - Define the linits that any feasible solution **must** adhere to in order to be acceptable (pass/fail conditions, range limits, etc.)

### Example: My desk job is boring and pays like shit
1. Need Statement: My current job is unfulfilling and does not provide adequate compensation.
2. Improved job satisfaction and financial stability.
3. Objectives:
    - Find a new job what offers more engaging work within the next three months.
    - Secure a salary increase of at least 20% compared to my current compensation.
4. Constraints:
    - The new job must be within a commutable distance from my home.
    - The new job must offer health benefits comparable to or better than my current job.

### Example: I like the sea, but live inland
1. Need Statement: I want to experience living near the ocean but currently reside inland.
2. Goal Statement: Move to a coastal city to enjoy the benefits of living near the sea.
3. Objectives:
    - Research and identify a set of at least three and no more than five coastal cities that I would consider moving to within the next two months.
    - Save at least 15% of my current annual income to fund the move within the next year.
4. Constraints:
    - I hate Florida
    - The cost of living in the new city must not exceed 120% of my current cost of living.
    - The new location must have a job market that aligns with my career goals and skills.

## How to Approach Framing Your Document
### Understand the Need
Does someone need more info? Are current docs/guidelines inadequate? Have circumstances changed?
### Establish a Goal
Are you trying to educate? help someone complete a task or make a decision? Persuade someone to take action?
### Define Objectives
What form will the communication take? What will it look like? What research will it contain?
### Identify Constraints
Is there a deadline? Must you use specific resources? How will your readers access the document?
