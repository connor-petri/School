# Lecture 4: Cover Letters

---

## Why Wrtite a Cover Letter If You Aren't Required To?
- According to 2023 survey, 
    - 83% of employers believe knowing how to write a cover letter is an important skill for job seekers 
    - 45% of employers say they are less likely to interview a candidate who doesn't submit a cover letter
    - 77% of recruiters say they give preference to candidates who submit a cover letter, even if it's not required
- A cover letter is an opportunity to:
    - Introduce yourself to the employer and explain why you are interested in the position.
    - Highlight your relevant skills, experiences, and accomplishments that make you a strong candidate for the job.
    - Demonstrate your communication skills and professionalism.

## Basic Principles
- Don't send a "general" cover letter - always write a new one tailored to the job you are applying for
- Don't just repeat information on your resume in the letter
- Don't write too formally or too informally
- Do demonstrate
    - That you know what the job entails
    - That you meet the requirements of the job
    - That you have relevant experience and skills

## Structure of a Cover Letter
### Header
- Your name, address, phone number, and email address
- Date

### Salutation
- Dear [Hiring Manager's Name],
- If you don't know the name, you can use "Dear Hiring Manager," or "Dear [Company Name] Recruitment Team,"

### Introduction
- State the position you are applying for and how you found out about it.
- State your education and/or current job title if relevant.
- Briefly explain why you are interested in the position and the company.

### Body
- Highlight your relevant skills, experiences, and accomplishments that make you a strong candidate for the job
- Quantify your achievements with specific examples and numbers if possible
- Explain how your skills and experiences align with the requirements of the job

### Conclusion
- Reiterate your interest in the position and the company
- Thank the employer for considering your application
- Express your willingness to discuss your qualifications further in an interview

### Valediction
- Sincerely,
- Best regards,
- Thank you for your consideration,

## Dos and Don'ts
### Get the Basics Right
- Format the letter corectly
- Choose the right font and size
- Professional tone
- Proofread for spelling and grammar errors

### Show, don't tell
- Experience is more important than passion

### Don't Ramble
- No more than 1 page

### Focus on what they want, not what you want
- Mirror job specs
- Show how you meet those specs

## Prospecting Cover Letters
- A cover letter written speculatively to a company in the hopes of securing ajob or internship, even if there are no current job openings
- Can be effective in demonstrating initiative and interest in the company, but may have a lower response rate than cover letters for advertised positions
- Rhetoric has to shift from "I am a great fit for this job" to "Your company needs x and I can provide it"
