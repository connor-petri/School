## Sexual Dimorphism
Any traits that relate to reproduction. Sexual dimorphism is about 2 things at the same time:
- Competition
- Different parental investments.

Humans are not very sexually dimorphic compared to other primates.

### Primary Sex Charateristics
**Primary sex charateristics** are any traits that directly relate to sexual reproduction.
- Testes
- Genitalia
- Uterus
- Hormones

### Secondary Sex Characteristics
- **Secondary sex characteristics** are traits that differentiate sexes withing a species but are not directly involved in reproduction.
- *Secondary sex characteristics* are **sexually dimorphic** traits.
    - For example: breast size is an example of sexual dimorphism in humans.
    - Size, strength, nose size, and hair patterns are general examples.

## Two Pathways of Sexual Selection
- **Intrasexual competition** (Eliminate rivals)
    - Rival exclusion favors weapons and other traits that give an advantage in these competitions.

- **Intersexual choice** (mate preference)
    - Mate choice favors ornaments and displays.

There is a correlation between sexual dimorphism patterns and mating systems. The more sexual dimorphism is present, the more differences there are in parental care between the sexes.

## Why Did Humans Lose Their Canines?
- **Early hominin** show reduced canine dimorphism and reduced sexual dimorphism.
- Shift in sexual selection: less payoff to male-male contest weapons, more to mate choice of prosocial + offspring care.
-Rising paternal investment + cooperative breeding favored reliability and **male caregiving** (mothering) over risky weaponry.
- Male + female choice in caregiving ecology: preferences for prosocial, low-coercion partners.
- Genus **Homo** defined by rapid **decline** in **sexual dimorphism**.
