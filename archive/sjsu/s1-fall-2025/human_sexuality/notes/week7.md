## The Woman that Never Evolved
- **Polarized reception:** hailed as corrective to androcentric primatology; criticized as "sociobiology in feminist clothing" and "another woman that never existed"
- **Academic pushback:** discomfort with claims about **female sexuality**, competition, and coalition power; worry about "biological determinism."
- **Media Framing:** sensational headlines obscured Hrdy's **strategic/coalitional** argument.
- **Within anthropology:** Long term fieldworkers found her lens predictive for infanticide risk, paternity management, and female alliances.

### Hrdy's claim: females are not "coy"
- **Female primates are sexual strategists:**
    - Females are assertive, competitive, strategic; they choose, manipulate, and form coalitions.
    - Tactics include mate choice, paternity confusion, alliances to reduce coercion/infanticide.
    - "Extended" sexuality and concealed ovulation as tools.
- **New Agenda:** stop counting only male sexual aggression, start measuring **female-female bonding,** non-conceptive sex as socially functional -> understand human sexual activity.
- Reframed **sex** as part of a **social strategy;** not just for reproduction.
    - Diversified mating activity and female-coalitions -> highly adaptive.

### The "Coy" Female
- Came from Darwin: Sexually active males and coy females.
    - **Sexual selection** strategies competition vs mate choice framed **females** as **coy/choosy** and **males** as **ardent/eager/competitive.**
- **Victorian cultural backdrop:** women as modest,passive, men as active pursuers. Darwin essentially projected the societal expectations of the society he lived in onto his work.

### Feminist Critique
- **Central Problems**
    1. **Small/biased datasets,** simplified measures of reproductive success;
    2. **Category bias** in reproduction-focused metrics
    3. **Androcentric assumptions** interpreted through contemporary gender norms; fit people's cognitive bias.
- **Hrdy's/feminist critique:** primate fieldwork + feminist science show **female competition, coalitions, diversified mating** and sociosexual functions.

## Non-Conceptive Sex in Primates
- Sex that is not for the purpose of reproduction.
- In many primate species, most sexual activity is **non-conceptive**
    - *Big change* from earlier models: only acknowledged sex for reproduction.
    - In primates, **non-conceptive sex** often refers to same-sex sex or **homosexual** sex.
    - **Heterosexuality** *not always as common*; reserved for breeding season or fertility windows, sometimes for social reasons.
- **Documented functions:** bonding, tension reduction, coalition upkeep, securing allotments, negotiating access, protecting infants, etc.
    - **Analytical rule:** ask *what problem* the behavior is solving.

## Macaques
- Large group, different species, "old World Monkey", resides in Asia.
- **Matrilineal, matrilocal** -> females inherit mother's rank.
- Mostly female-dominated, multi-partner.
- Males never fully control social structure.
- **Seasonal F-F consortships** (same-sex pair bonds/relationships): courtship, mounting, grooming, guarding; sometimes exclusive for days, weeks.
    -Occurs **in the wild** (rates vary by troop/ecology); not a captivity artifact.
- **Female strategies:** bonding/coalition maintenance, tension reduction, access to feeding sites & social partners; females initiate, persist, coordinate.

## Gorillas
- **Male-dominated context**
    - Single silverback or small coalition controls access
    - Low sperm competition -> fewer males mating
    - Female leverage appears hidden.
- **Female Strategies**
    - **Direct tactics** they initiate, persist, and resist unwanted advances.
    - **Timeing** conceive when access to preferred male is favorable
    - **Group transfer** move to stronger silverback -> shapes paternity, offspring safety, tong-term outcomes.
    - **Social Bonds** form affiliative ties with other females -> alloparent support, buffering, minor coalition aid.

## From Primate to Human Sexuality
- Humans share primate roots, but express sexuality differently; debate
- **Humans** -> more frequent non-conceptive hetero-sex than other primates except for bonobos, in part because humans have sex year-round.
- Sexuality shaped by biology, culture, and evolution
- **Human uniqueness:** tendency toward faculative pair-bonds, language, symbolic culture.

## Historic Sexuality
### Victorians
- Orientation terms, categories, morals, and meanings shift culturally and historically.
- Victorian society praised modesty, restraint, rigid gender roles, which Darwin projected onto his work.
- Equated sex and gender.
    - Medicine inherited "**one sex model**" with the male body as the norm.
    - Female body = inverted, less developed version of male.
    - Vagina and ovaries were considered "inverted penis and balls"
    - This is where the "coy female" idea came from.

### 20th Century
- **Freud:** sexuality central to personality and development.
- **Kinsey:** continuum of sexual orientation; large-scale surveys.
- **Masters & Johnson:** physiology of sexual response.
- **Foucault:** Anthro/Social constructionist approaches argue that sexuality is shaped by culture and history.

#### Kinsey and the Study of Homosexuality
- Large-scale surveys: *Sexual Behavior in the Human Male*
- Revealed ***homosexuality*** *more common* than assumed.
- Introduced the Kinsey scale (0-6 continuum of sexual orientations).
- Challenged binary categories; emphasized variation and fluidity.
- Destigmatized same-sex behavior as part of natural human diversity.

#### Masters and Johnson: Physiology of Sexual Response
- Watched people have sex in a lab.
- Direct observation.
- **Identified 4 stages:** excitement, plateau, orgasm, resolution.
- Demonstrated physiological similarity in men and women.
- Helped destigmatize sexual science, informed sex therapy, **sexual dysfunction**, sexology.
- Master's had sex with Johnson, which is sexual assault/rape, but wasn't considered that at the time.

## Defining Sexual Orientation
- **Sexual Orientation** - enduring patterns of emotional/sexual attraction.
    - Kinsey: orientation is not binary and exists on a continuum.
- **Main Categories**
    - **Heterosexuality** - opposite sex attraction
    - **Homosexuality** - attraction to the same sex
    - **Bisexuality** attraction to more than one sex
    - **Pansexuality** attraction regardless of sex or gender identity
    - **Asexuality** - No sexual attraction

## Human Sexuality Key Definitions
- **Sex** imagined biological categories of attributes (chromosomes, anatomy, hormones)
    - chromosomal sex vs. anatomical sex; intersex
- **Gender** imagined social and cultural roles, expectations, identities.
    - Gender roles vary by culture; third genders; trans vs cisgender.
- **Sexuality** attraction, behavior, identity, orientation.
    - Identity $\neq$ behavior $\neq$ attraction

## Genetic Basis of Sexual Orientation: Studies
- **Twin studies:** higher concordance among identical twins.
- **Genome studies** no "**gay gene**" but multiple contributing loci, continuous traits.
- Prenatal hormones may influence brain development.
- Biology provides predispositions, not strict determinism.

## Environmental and Developmental Influences.
- **Birth order effect** later-born brothers more likely to be gay.
    - "fraternal birth order effect"
- Family dynamics and early environment *may* shape outcomes
- Cultural context influences identity and self0expression
- Orientation emerges from culture + biology interaction *biocultural approach*
