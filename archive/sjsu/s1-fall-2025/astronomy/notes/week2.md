## Backgound Information
### Distance from Earth:
- Moon: 1 ls

The universe is fucking massive. It is so large that the **nearest galaxy** to the milky way is 2.5 million light years away. This means when we observe Andromeda, we are seeing it as it was 2.5 million years ago. This essentially means that the farther into space we look, the farther back in time we can observe. 1 ly = 9.4607e+12 km.

### Earth's movement through the Solar System
- Earth is moving in several ways in relation to the sun. It is spinning about it's axis. This means stuff on the equator is moving faster relative to the Sun compared to someone at the pole. 
- The Earth is also orbiting the Sun. The time this takes is called it's **orbital period**. The Earth orbits at an average of 1 AU (~150 km) and it's axis is tilted at 22.5 degrees. This is processional, meaning the direction it tilts is changing in a cyclictic manner, meaning Polaris won't always be the North Star.


## Seasons
- The seasons occur due to different lengths of exposure to the Sun caused by the Earth's axial tilt, which stays almost the same relative to the universe during its orbit.
- The **summer solstice** is the longest day of the year (in terms of sunlight), and the **winter solstice** is the shortest day of the year.
