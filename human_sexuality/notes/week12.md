## Selection Frameworks
- How do we evaluate if different sexual orientations, diversity, and identities are **adaptive?**
- **Frameworks:**
    - **Sexual selection**
    - **inclusive fitness and kin selection**
    - **Balancing selection**
    - **Antagonistic pleiotropy**

### Genetic Frameworks
- **Pleiotropy** means one gene can influence several traits
- **Polygenic** means many genes with small effects shape one trait

### Continuous Traits
- **Sexual orientation** is a **continuous trait** with overlapping distributions, not binary or discrete categories
- **Expression is shaped by **environment** and **culture** which can *amplify or mute biology*
    - helps explain why, despite a primate capacity for same-sex behavior, self reported homosexual identity minority in humans

## Intersex
- **Sex is also continuous:**
    - /w selection nudging populations toward more **masculine** or **feminine** averages
    - some traits show strong sex differentiation because **diversifying selection** pushes the sexes apart
- Other traits show **stabilizing selection** that pulls the populations toward an average; height illustrates this pattern
    - expect many small effects spread across the genome with some sex differences in genetic architecture and correlations with other traits
