## Different Sexual Orientationsas an Alloparental Strategy
- **Same sex pairs** can act as alloparents when mates are scarce or parents die
- This can be adaptive in social species with costly children and few births
- **For humans,** broaden "designated alloparent" to include sexual diverse orientations and identities
- How does this give an advantage evolutionarily

## Selection Frameworks
- How do we evaluate if different sexual orientations, diversity, and identities are **adaptive?**
- **Frameworks:**
    - **Sexual selection**
    - **inclusive fitness and kin selection**
    - **Balancing selection**
    - **Antagonistic pleiotropy**

### Genetic Frameworks
- **Pleiotropy** means one gene can influence several traits
- **Polygenic** means many genes with small effects shape one trait

### Continuous Traits
- **Sexual orientation** is a **continuous trait** with overlapping distributions, not binary or discrete categories
- **Expression is shaped by **environment** and **culture** which can *amplify or mute biology*
    - helps explain why, despite a primate capacity for same-sex behavior, self reported homosexual identity minority in humans

## Intersex
- **Sex is also continuous:**
    - /w selection nudging populations toward more **masculine** or **feminine** averages
    - some traits show strong sex differentiation because **diversifying selection** pushes the sexes apart
- Other traits show **stabilizing selection** that pulls the populations toward an average; height illustrates this pattern
    - expect many small effects spread across the genome with some sex differences in genetic architecture and correlations with other traits

## Transgender
- ~1% of the population is estimated to be trans
- **Mental sexual differentiation** happens later in life than physical
    - Transgender brains have been proven to be more similar to the sex they feel most drawn to
- **Standard view** treats **sexual diversity** as a by-product of the heterosexual pair-bond
    - reframe humans as cooperative breeders who rely on *designated alloparents* for child survival
- **Inclusive fitness** and **kin selection + antagonistic pleiotropy** predict **sexual diversity** can raise lineage success through targeted kin help

### Inclusive Fitness
- Asks if **variation** in **sexual orientation and identity** could be *adaptive, rather than side effects*
    - similar to **grandmother hypothesis** questions
- **Adaptive prediction:** sexually diverse individuals show lower direct reproduction paired w/ regular help to nieces and nephews more than unrelated children
- Measure **Avuncular tendencies** toward nieces and nephews:
    - **Avuncular tendencies** = uncle tendencies or male alloparenting
    - focused studied use *third gender** cases - AMAB sibling who adopts a feminine role
    - \+ male androphilia - AMAB + attracted to men
- Androphilic males have fewer direct children, trait persists at steady levels across populations; persistence suggests adaptive advantage, likely thu benefits to close kin.
