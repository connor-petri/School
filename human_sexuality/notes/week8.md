## Mate Choice and Sexual Attraction
- **Mate Choice** - selecting partners for reproduction, bonding, or companionship.
- **Sexual attraction** - feelings of desire/arousal toward another person; desire for physical, sexual contact or intimacy; "hotness"
    - **Romantic Attraction** - involves emotional desire for connection, intimacy, and romantic relationship.
- Attraction is not mate choice (feelings vs. selection)
- Applies across orientations
- Shaped by both **evolution** and **culture**
- **Unconscious cues** and **conscious values**
    - Aesthetics and prosocial traits important.

### Origins of Mate Choice Theory
#### Darwin (He is incorrect)
- **Two mechanisms**
    - Male-male competition.
    - Mate choice (preference for specific traits)

#### Psychological and Evolutionary Frameworks
- **Trivers' Parental Investment theory** - sex investing more in offspring becomes choosier.
- **Buss' Provider Model** - Women prefer men with resources; men prefer youth and fertility.
    - Controversial & problematic (Buss is the worst)
- Sets the stage for modern debates in human mate choice research.

## Secondary Sex Characteristics & Human Mate Choice
- **Human evolutionary shift: loss of large canines** -> selection away from competition, coercive aggression; **prosociality,** reliability, empathy, caregiving become attractive.
- Multi-cue, multi-sense integration of **weak signals:** body shape, face, voice, scent, movement.
### Evaluating Attractiveness
#### Unconscious "fast" layer: 
- rapid, automatic appraisal (ms response time)
- Flags relevant cues as salient = vibes?
- **Location:** subcortical appraisal-and-reward circuit, especially *nucleus accumbens*.
- **Key Chemicals:** dopamine, beta-endorphins, oxytocin/vasopressin
- **Lust -> attraction -> attachment** (overlapping not linear)

#### Conscious "slow" layer
- **Prefrontal Cortex** is the "thinky part"
    - **Executive functions:** critical thinking, planning, decision-making, problem-solving, working memory, attention impulse control, social cognition, ect.
    - **Top-down control** over reward circuits **override/amplify** first-pass attraction.
- **Cultural learning** reshapes appraisal: social ideals, moral norms, ect.

## Standards of Attractiveness (Core Cues)
- **Unconscious attraction cues:** body shape, face, skin, movements, voice, scent
    - **Visual:** vody shape, facial symmetry, **averageness**, skin clarity/texture, youth-linked features.
    - **Movement (dynamic cues):** hip sway/shoulder swagger, posture and gait, speed dependant.
    - **Auditory:** voice pitch/formants
    - **Olfactory:** odor/genetic-compatibility.
    - **Cultural backgrounds** and **experiences** can influence outcomes.


## Female Secondary Sex Characteristics & Attractiveness
- **Female waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) = 0.7** is repeatedly preferred in ratings and **eye-tracking** studies; first fixation target is the **waist/breasts** then **hips**.
- **WHR** linked to hormones, fertility, and health, functional rational for why this proportion may be unconsciously prioritized.

## Male Secondary Sex Characteristics & Attractiveness
- **Male waist-to-chest ratio (WCR)** preverence for a pronounced **shoulder-to-hip "V-shape"**; Also 0.7.
- **Additional weak cues commonly studied:**
    - **Voice:** lower fundamental frequency increases attractiveness/dominance in short term; warmth/trust cues.
    - **Scent:** genetic and immune compatibility
    - **Facial hair:** light stubble frequently preferred

## Same-sex & Bisexual Attraction
- **Gay Men:** strong visual weighting; preferences often include **leanness, muscularity, Vshape,** and face/skin cues.
- **Lesbian Women:** broader variation; attractiveness judgments often integrate **warmth, competence,** and **style/grooming/gender identity** with body/face cues
- **Bisexuality:** evidence of **fluidity** across time/context; patterns can shift with relationship goals.
