## The Woman that Never Evolved
- **Polarized reception:** hailed as corrective to androcentric primatology; criticized as "sociobiology in feminist clothing" and "another woman that never existed"
- **Academic pushback:** discomfort with claims about **female sexuality**, competition, and coalition power; worry about "biological determinism."
- **Media Framing:** sensational headlines obscured Hrdy's **strategic/coalitional** argument.
- **Within anthropology:** Long term fieldworkers found her lens predictive for infanticide risk, paternity management, and female alliances.

### Hrdy's claim: females are not "coy"
- **Female primates are sexual strategists:**
    - Females are assertive, competitive, strategic; they choose, manipulate, and form coalitions.
    - Tactics include mate choice, paternity confusion, alliances to reduce coercion/infanticide.
    - "Extended" sexuality and concealed ovulation as tools.
- **New Agenda:** stop counting only male sexual aggression, start measuring **female-female bonding,** non-conceptive sex as socially functional -> understand human sexual activity.
- Reframed **sex** as part of a **social strategy;** not just for reproduction.
    - Diversified mating activity and female-coalitions -> highly adaptive.

### The "Coy" Female
- Came from Darwin: Sexually active males and coy females.
    - **Sexual selection** strategies competition vs mate choice framed **females** as **coy/choosy** and **males** as **ardent/eager/competitive.**
- **Victorian cultural backdrop:** women as modest,passive, men as active pursuers. Darwin essentially projected the societal expectations of the society he lived in onto his work.

### Feminist Critique
- **Central Problems**
    1. **Small/biased datasets,** simplified measures of reproductive success;
    2. **Category bias** in reproduction-focused metrics
    3. **Androcentric assumptions** interpreted through contemporary gender norms; fit people's cognitive bias.
- **Hrdy's/feminist critique:** primate fieldwork + feminist science show **female competition, coalitions, diversified mating** and sociosexual functions.

## Non-Conceptive Sex in Primates
- Sex that is not for the purpose of reproduction.
- In many primate species, most sexual activity is **non-conceptive**
    - *Big change* from earlier models: only acknowledged sex for reproduction.
    - In primates, **non-conceptive sex** often refers to same-sex sex or **homosexual** sex.
    - **Heterosexuality** *not always as common*; reserved for breeding season or fertility windows, sometimes for social reasons.
- **Documented functions:** bonding, tension reduction, coalition upkeep, securing allotments, negotiating access, protecting infants, etc.
    - **Analytical rule:** ask *what problem* the behavior is solving.

## Bonobos
- **Female-dominated context: Parish's classic study** on **female-female bonding** bia grooming, alliances, and **GG-rubbing**
    - Coalitions allow access to food, reduce male coercion; female dominance possible.
### Video 1: Bonobos eating with GG-Rubbing

### Video 2: Female Bonobo Coalition
- Female bonobos form tight alliances to protect themselves, maintain peace, and exert power over males.

## Macaques
- Large group, different species, "old World Monkey", resides in Asia.
- **Matrilineal, matrilocal** -> females inherit mother's rank.
- Mostly female-dominated, multi-partner.
- Males never fully control social structure.
- **Seasonal F-F consortships** (same-sex pair bonds/relationships): courtship, mounting, grooming, guarding; sometimes exclusive for days, weeks.
    -Occurs **in the wild** (rates vary by troop/ecology); not a captivity artifact.
- **Female strategies:** bonding/coalition maintenance, tension reduction, access to feeding sites & social partners; females initiate, persist, coordinate.
