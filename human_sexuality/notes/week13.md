## The Omegaverse
- The omegaverse came out of a multitide of sources that came together in the 2010s
- Since then it has taken a multitude of variations as diverse as the authors writing.
- Often involves (disproved) wolf and/or werewolf vocabulary (alpha, beta, omega, etc.)

### The "Rules" of the Omegaverse
- There are as many versions to the Omegaverse as there are fics written on it
- This versatility allows authors a large amount of freedom within the confines of the trope.
- Still there are some key "rules" which can still be bent and nevertheless make an Omegaverse fic
    - Sometimes rooted in making men experience things they usually wouldn't, for example misogyny or pregnancy
- Individuals are increasingly more free to play with and express their gender in unique ways, we see this play with concepts in the variation of fics within the genre

### Tropes
- Must contain "alpha", "beta", and "Omega"
- **Gender Hierarchies**
    - Make men experience downsides of being marginalized
- Sex is often a key aspect of Omegaverse fics, but isn't a requirement
- **Gender bending** - Women as Alphas and Men as ***BREEDABLE*** Omegas
- **Power Dynamics** Gendered roles often included and enforced, often satirically
- **Pregnancy & Abortion** are frequent topics
- **Biological Essentialism** - Characters lose control of their bodies due to overwhelming sexual drive, this often dive to non-consensual topics being explore.
- **Arranged Marriage** - a frequent trope that plays into power dynamics and codified misogyny

### The Hays Code
- The Hays Code was a way for Hollywood to self regulate via a self imposed set of rules, rather than have to deal with a government run legal body to control rules and enforcement
- Homosexuality ws mostly absent initially, however in the 60s some rules were relaxed. This brought a different set of issues. While homosexuality could now be included it had to be shown to be problematic and a detriments, which frequently ended in the tragic death for queer characters.
- These rules are no longer enforced, but their legacy continues to impact media today

## Destiel (Supernatural Ship) as a Case Study in Queer Bating
- **Queerbaiting** is when a TB show or movie introduces sexual tension between two characters and uses romantic tropes but refuses to follow through on the relationship.
- Supernatural's writers added jokes poking fun at Dean's sexual orientation for fun
- This humor was adopted by fans
- Initially audiences focused on **"Wincest"** (Winchester Incest)
