## Concealed Ovulation
### Estrus and Ovulation
- **Estrus**: the fertile phase when females are sexually receptive and attractive to males
    - Can be both behavioral and physiological
- **Ovulation**: the release of an egg from the ovary, marking the fertile period in the menstrual cycle
    - **Hormones:** FSH and LH from the pituitary gland in brain
    - After ovulation, corpus luteum; **progesterone** becomes dominant = **luteal phase**

### Swelling in Apes
- Most Old World monkeys show *sexual swellings* during estrus
- In chimps and bonobos, genital skil enlarges/swells and reddens/pinks near ovulation
- Gorillas show much less dramatic, but still present genital swelling and behavioral changes
- Visible fertility cues shape male competition and female choice

### Concealed Ovulation
- Hidden Estrus: fertile females show no obvious external changes that reveal when ovulation happens
- Humans have concealed ovulation: no obvious external signs of fertility

## Orangutans
- **Similarities to Humans**:
    - Complex tool use and local culture
        - Documented use of medicinal herbs
    - Very slow life history
    - Highly dependent offspring
    - High cognitive capacity

### Social System and Life History
- Solitary but semi-social
- Adult females hold overlapping home ranges, usually with their offspring and other males
- One large resident male's range can include several female ranges
- Very long interbirth intervals and *long* juvenile dependence
    - Wild fathers provide little to no direct care

### Sexual Selection Strategy: Male Bimaturism
- Adult males show two morphs:
    - Flanged males are large with cheek pads and throat sacs
    - Unflanged males are adults, but lack full secondary traits
- Development of flanges can be delayed for many years, unpredictable; not a factor of age

#### Flanged Males
- Larger and dominant type
- **Call and wait** sexual strategy with long calls + mate guarding and protection
- Long vocal calls to attract fertile females who travel to their area
- More successful when females can choose

#### Unflanged Males
- Smaller and non-dominant; underdeveloped and avoid direct confrontation
- "Sneak-and-rape" reproductive strategy
- Forced copulations and harassment function as mating route

### Female Concealed Ovulation
- Show menstrual cycles, but no external signs of fertility
- Females mate with several males causing paternity confusion
- Female orangutans use strategies that express agency in a context of frequent coercion

## No Consensus on Reason for Concealed Ovulation
- Four main "schools of thought"
    1. **Continuous sexual receptivity and rape as adaptive** (problematic)
    2. **Baby-trapping:** human females evolved concealed ovulation to baby-trap mates
    3. **Cuckoldry:** ovulation in a mostly monogamous system lets females seek extra-pair mates during fertile days while keeping investment from primary partner
    4. **Feminist Critique:** varied, but sometimes frames other schools of thought as problematic

### Feminist Critique of Rape as Adaptive
- Emphasized multiple lines of evidence fail to support **rape adaptation models**; rape in humans not a specific adaptation
- Argued that this theory was dangerous in perpetuating rape culture

### Byproduct Accounts
- Byproduct and constriant theory views loss of estrus signals linjed to bipedalism, anatomy, and clothing/cultural adaptations

### Female Rivalry Hypothesis
- Concealed ovulation may have evolved to avoid aggression from other females, not to manipulate males.
- Concealining fertile windows reduces chances for rivals to sabotage matings or status;
    - Shifts attention from male investment to female-female competition for male mates
    - Set up an agent-based world model of "concealers" vs "revealers"
    - 10,000 independent runs of the model
- Challenges the idea that male provisioning and the nuclear family are neutral
