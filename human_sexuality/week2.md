### Natural Selection
**Natural selection** refers to the process by which organism arise and develop through the selection of small, inherited variaions ("descent with modification") that increase an organism's ability to preferentially survive and reproduce.
    - "Units of inheritance" = "gemmules."
    - "Struggle for existance."
    - Mechanism of evolution.

**Adaptive radiation** - The diversification of an ancestral group of organisms over time when changes to environment occur.\

### Sexual Selection
- **Sexual selection** is a type of selection that arised as the result of the differential reproductive success that arised from competition for mates and access to fertilization.


## Two Main Mechanisms of Sexual Selection:
### Intrasexual selection
- Within/among a sex (m vs m or f vs f).
- Favors secondary sexual traits.
- Think competitions and denial of resources.
### Intersexual selection
- Selection between sexes.
- Think mating rituals, dances, and pattern.
- Females are not idley consuming these rituals, they are processing social information and performing risk assessment.

It is important to know that Darwin's view of this ignores the fact that females of some species compete.

### Inheritane Timing and Sex-Limited Expression
- **Sex-Limited Expressions:**
    - Late-developing traits -> often sex-limited to either m or f (e.g antlers).
    - Early-developing traits -> more often shared by both sexes.
    - Something about sexual dimorphism

### Scrambles and Endurance Rivalry
- **Scramble competitions**:** race to locate mates quickly.
- **Endurance rivalry:** Persistance at display sites.
- **Traits:** speed, sensory acuity, spatial memory; stamina condition residency
- **Predictions:** male-biased OSR -> more time = more mating
- **Female agency:** sampling strategies & information use.

### Post-Copulatory Selection
- **Post-copulatory selection** is selection that continues after mating.
    - **Sperm competition:** ejaculates from multiple males overlap and compete to fertilize the female's egg.
    - **Cryptic female choice** female biology biases fertilization. Usually hidden physically, chemically, and biologically. Can be used to prevent unknown paternity.
    - **Sexual Coercion** - force/harassment to increase mating access.
    - **Infanticide** - takeover tactic to shorten interbirth interval.
    - **Female Counter-strategies** - coalitions, mate switching, paternity confusion.

## Red Queen Hypothesis
- The **Red Queen Hypothesis** proposes that organisms must constantly adapt and evolve not just for reproductive advantage but also to survive against ever-evolving opposing organisms (e.g., predators, parasites).
- "It takes all the running you can do, to stay in the same place."
- Essentially organisms need to keep evolving or they will go extinct.

## Measuring Sexual Selection
### Four ways of measuring sexual selection:
1. Parental invenstment (PI)
2. Operational sex ration (OSR)
3. Potential reproductive rate (PPR)
4. Bateman gradient

### Parental Investment
Any investment by a parent in an offspring that increases that offspring's chances of survival, at the cost of the parent's ability to ivest in other offspring.

#### PI in young is marked by:
- Longer childhood
- birth spacing
- learned bahavior
- low birth numbers

#### Forms:
- Gestation, lactation. guarding, feeding, play, ect.
- Higher PI: sex is usually choosier.
- Lower PI: sex usually competes.
- PI is flexible across species and varies with ecology.

### Operational Sex Ratio
$$OSR = \frac{\text{Ready to Mate Males}}{\text{Fertile Females}}$$
#### OSR is shaped by:
- Season length
- Syncronization of female fertility
- Time spent on care.
