## The Provisioning Hypothesis
The **provisioning hypothesis** proposed to *infer* mating systems and sexual psychologies from locomotion.
- Coined by **C. Owen Lovejoy** 
- Bipedalism -> hands free -> food transport -> regular provisioning.
- **Provisioning** boosts female reproductive success -> favors **monogamous pair-bond**.
- Investment payoff hinges on **paternal certainty; pair-bonding/mate-guarding** raise expected genetic returns.
- **Outcomes:** 
    - Reduced male-male competition, shifts in **sexual dimorphism**.
    - Pair-bonds stabilize, one to few females (tendency towards polygyny).
    - **Family unit** becomes key selection arena; **sexual division of labor (SDL)** emerges as complementary roles.

#### Energetics and Life History: Why Provisioning Could Pay
- Maternal constraints: **pregnancy, lactation,** infant transport -> reduced foraging radius.
- **Distance-payload** trade-off: male transport adds **calories;** predicable **home base** or rendezvous point -> **sexual division of labor.**
- **Potential effects:** shorter interbirth intervals, higher infant survivorship, paternal certainty, pair-bonding. 

#### How Provisioning Connects to Sexual Division of Labor
- **Sexual division of labor** as adaptive complementarity: male **procure/transport**, female **gestate/lactate/care**
- **Visibility effect:** procurement looks **decisive/public;** care looks **continuous/private**

### Afterlife of the Provisioning Hypothesis
- **Late 90's-2010's:** confidence **erodes** as single-cause for bipedalism; multiple alternatives + evidence questioned.
- **2009-2010 "Ardi" papers:** pair-bond provisioning resurgence, but no wide return to consensus.
- **Provisioning** -> one pathway of many; **pair-bond** narrative remains **repackaged** in popular texts
    - **Example:** Gray & Garcia textbook foregrounds the pair-bond.

#### Why the Pair-Bond Persists
- Fits cultural **biases;** offers a neat causal chain.
- **Sticky Stories:** simple, memorable, and "stick" in minds.
- **Zombie theories:** critiqued, but refuted to die. (*Man the Hunter*, Laetoli couple dimorphism).


## Feminist Critique: What Provision Smuggles In
- Treats **family (heterosexual pair + dependent child) as the **selection unit**.
- Moves from **what can be done** (transport) -> to **who must do it** (men).

#### Parental Certainty
- Assumes **paternal certainty** to justify costly male investment.
- **Cross-cultural variation:** paternal investment ranges from **intense direct care** to **indirect** support or **olow** presence.
    - **Decenter paternal certainty;** why make **assured paternity** the gateway to infant food when **cooperative care** already moves calories?
    - Paternity assurance can **constrain** women's autonomy; ignores **networked** resource flows.
