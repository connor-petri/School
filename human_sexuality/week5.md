## Discovery of Laetoli

- Laetoli footprints discovered in Tanzania, dating back 3.6 million years.
- Evidence of bipedalism in early hominins.
- Footprints suggest group movement, possibly family units.
- The "pair-bonded" story told of them is a cultural projection of the *nuclear family*.

## The Nuclear Family

- An idealized, patriarchal family stucture characterized ny a working father, stay-at-home mother, and their children.
- Sexual division of labor is a key characteristic.

## Gender Roles in Evolution?
### Man the Hunter
- A textbook that taught that hunting was done by males.
- Believed the hunter was the driving force in human evolution.
- All human developments are because man go hunt ooga booga.
- The precursor to Man the Provider.

#### Woman the Gatherer
- A feminist critique and rewriting of Man the Hunter.
- Emphasizes the cooperative nature of evolution, but maintains the gender roles of it's predecessor.

#### Showoff Hypothesis
- Proposed that women brought in more calories by gathering and men only hunted to show off.
- Still maintained men were the only one to hunt.

### HOLY SHIT WOMEN HUNTED???
- Yes dumb old men lied. Just like in every other field.
- Women hunters with children do not experience a negative impact in hunting efficiency compared to women without children.
- Women hunted when menstruating.
- Women hunted while nursing children.
- The Grandmother Hypothesis redistributes the burdens of childcare.
- When Women and Men work together, they collect more food.

#### Wait so where did the idea that only men hunt?
- Hunting only became about gender during the 1950s. Before that it was about class.
- This was a expression of status rather than being about survival.
- Post WWII, the nuclear family and gender roles promoted hunting and other outdoor activities as masculine.
- Coincided with the rise of "primitive masculinity."
