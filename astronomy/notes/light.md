## What is Light?
- Light is ***electromagnetic radiation***.
- It can act as both a particle and a wave.
- Particles of light are called ***photons***.
- Shorter wavelength -> more energy.
- **Wien's Law**: All objects emit EM Waves.

### Types of Waves
In order from shortest wavelength to longest:
- Gamma Rays
- X-Rays
- Ultraviolet Rays
- Visible Light
- Infrared Radiation
- Microwaves
- Radio Waves


### Doppler Effect
- a.k.a blue shift and red shift
- If an object is moving away from an observer, the wavelength of the light it emits becomes longer, shifting the light towards infrared.
- Conversely, if an object is moving towards an observer, the wavelength of the light it emits shortens, "blue shifting" it
