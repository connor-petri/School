## Light

### What is Light?
- Light is ***electromagnetic radiation***.
- It can act as both a particle and a wave.
- Particles of light are called ***photons***.
- Shorter wavelength -> more energy.
- **Wien's Law**: All objects emit EM Waves.

#### Types of Waves
In order from shortest wavelength to longest:
- Gamma Rays
- X-Rays
- Ultraviolet Rays
- Visible Light
- Infrared Radiation
- Microwaves
- Radio Waves
