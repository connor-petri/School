## Astronomical History

### Aristotle
- Coined the geocentric model of the universe.
- This model could not account for apparent retrograde motion.

### Copernicus
- Refined the heliocentric model.
- Model was still woefully inaccurate as he assumed the planets moved in perfect circles.

### Tycho Brahe
- Had a dwarf jester named Jepp who sat under his table at dinner.
- Also owned an elk lmao.
- Hired Johannes Kepler as a data analyst, but was super possessive of his information which led to strain into their professional relationship.
- Eventually entrusted his data to Kepler on his deathbed.

### Johannes Kepler
- Coined the 3 laws of planetary motion.
- Was so correct that it was proven by Newton more than a century later when he coined his laws of motion.

## Telescopes
### Refracting Telescope
- Essentially a giant light bucket that just collects the raw light from the sky and focuses it through lenses.
- Needs to be very ling with many very large and heavy lenses.

### Reflecting Telescope
- Giant light bucket but with mirrors for focusing and magnifying.
- 

### Resolution
- A telescope's resolution is the measure of how much detail it's images are. 
- There are 2 main ways to increase resolution:
    - Bigger telescope
    - Short wavelengths of light
- We can make multiple telescopes act as one using a process called *interferometry*.
