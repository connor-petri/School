## Dark Matter
An undetected form of mass that emits little or no light but whose existence we infer from its gravitational influence

## Evidence of the Big Bang

### Hubble's Law
- Observed galaxies and plotted them on a speed vs. distance from earth graph
- Found a positive linear correlation between speed and distance from the earth
    - The further the galaxy is from Earth, the faster they are moving relative to Earth
    - This is due to the expansion of space rather than a traditional force
- When solving for time using distance and velocity, every galaxy gives the same number of around 14 billion years

### Abundance of Atoms
- The abundance of H, He, and Li in the universe is due to the big bang
- The universe was hot enough and at a high enough pressure for fusion to occur for the first 3 minutes of its existence

### Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation
- In theory, you should be able to look up at the sky and see the big bang
- The EMF from the big bang has red shifted past the infrared and into the microwave range
- AT&T engineers observed these microwaves no matter which way they pointed their instruments

### Globular Clusters
- The oldest structures in the galaxy
- You can date them using the HR diagram
- The oldest of these are 11 billion years old
    - This fits into the estimated big bang timeline

### Quasars
- Super-massive black holes feeding in the early universe
- With a few exceptions the required mass-density for quasars to exist only occur in the early universe

### Irregular Galaxies
- Galaxies colliding
- Happened all the time in the early universe compared to now

### Olber's Paradox
- If the universe is infinite, then every line of sight should end on a star
- If this is true, then why is the sky dark at night?
    - The light has been red shifted by both time and the expansion of space
