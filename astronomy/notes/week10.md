## Black Holes

### Chandrasekhar
- First proposed by Chandrasekhar
- He had to leave England because he was harassed by his colleagues for his hypothesis.
- Came to USA and taught at the University of Chicago
- Awarded Nobel Prize 25 years after his initial proposal after black holes were confirmed to exist.

#### How did he discover it?
- White Dwarfs
    - Remains of a nova
    - Much denser than a normal star
    - Made of carbon - carbon + pressure = diamonds
- Neutron Star
    - Chandrasekhar thought about what would happen if the white dwarf ate another star.
    - Denser than white dwarfs - more mass and smaller radius
- Black Holes
    - When a neutron star is compressed beyond it's Schwarzschild Radius, it becomes a black hole as light can no longer escape it's gravitational pull.
    - The mass becomes a *singularity*


### Cygnus X1
- Binary O class and black hole system
- Black hole is eating the star
- Black has a massive accretion disk around it
- The magnetic field generated by the movement of charged particles in the accretion disk form jets of charged particles at the poles.

### Gravitational Lensing
- The gravity generated by a black hole is strong enough to bend light in a very apparent way, causing the universe appear warped around it
- This is called gravitational lensing, and all large gravitational bodies cause it, just at a smaller scale
    - The sun has an observable gravitational lensing effect if you have the right tools
