## Fusion and Fission
### Fission
- The splitting of atoms which results in the release of energy.
- Nuclear power plants and bombs work this way.

### Fusion
- The fusing of atoms which results in the release of energy.
- Stars fuse elements which is responsible for the energy they release.
- We are working on developing power plants that use fusion, but we have yet to crack the challenge of getting more energy out of fusion than we put in.
